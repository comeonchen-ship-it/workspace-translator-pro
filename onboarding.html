Step 2: Configure Extension Settings

Open the extension and paste your API key into Settings. This takes less than 1 minute.

1

Open the Extension Panel

Click the Workspace Translator Pro icon in your Chrome toolbar to open the side panel.

2

Open Settings

Click the Settings (⚙️) gear icon in the top right corner of the panel.

3

Choose Your Engine & Paste API Key

Select your translation provider from the dropdown, then paste the API key you obtained in Step 1. If using Free Google Translate, simply select it — no key needed.

4

Click “Test Connection” to Verify

After entering your API key and model name, click the Test Connection button. The status badge will update:
• ● 未驗證 · Unverified — not yet tested
• ● 生效 · Active — key is valid and working ✓
• ● 失效 · Invalid — key is incorrect or model not found

If the test fails, the result message will show the exact error and a suggestion on how to fix it.

5

Click “Save Configurations”

Your settings are saved locally in your browser and never sent to any external server. The extension will not allow translation to start if the API key status is not Active.

💡 Tip: Best Free Option

We recommend Gemini API for the best free experience — up to 1,500 requests/day and 30 requests/minute at no cost. This is usually enough to translate many large documents daily.

Step 3: Translate Your First Document!

You are ready! Here's how to run your first translation.

You're All Set!

Follow these steps to translate your first file:

Open any Google Slides, Docs, Sheets, or Forms document in a browser tab.
Click the Workspace Translator Pro icon to open the side panel — the document will be detected automatically.
Select your target language from the dropdown.
Click "Translate & Copy". On first use, Google will ask you to authorize access — click Allow.
A fully translated copy will open in a new tab automatically. Your original is never modified.

⚠️ First-Time Google Authorization

When you translate for the first time, a Google authorization window will appear. This is normal — it grants the extension access to copy and modify files on your behalf. You may see an "unverified app" warning: click "Advanced" → "Go to Workspace Translator Pro (unsafe)" to proceed. This happens because the app is new and hasn't completed Google's verification process yet.
